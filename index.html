Sunny Image Technology

Notes from a lab that looks at pictures for a living.

I work on computer vision and image processing — mostly on the unglamorous end: objects too small to see clearly, domains that shift under the model, labels too expensive to collect.

The research section now holds 51 papers across 11 areas, from domain adaptation and camouflaged object detection to knowledge distillation, active learning and image restoration. The project list runs from 2000; the course section keeps the notes for the two courses I teach; the reading lists cover the journals and conferences worth following, the mathematics behind the models, and the tools and datasets that actually get used.

Nothing here is a product. It is a notebook, kept in the open, mainly for my own students. If you find something useful, take it.
