Sunny Image Technology

Notes from a lab that looks at pictures for a living.

I work on computer vision and image processing — mostly on the unglamorous end: objects that are too small, domains that shift under the model, labels that are too expensive to collect. This site collects what I do in research, the projects I have been involved in since 2000, the two courses I teach, and the reading lists I hand to new students — the journals and conferences worth following, the mathematics behind the models, and the tools and datasets that actually get used.

Nothing here is a product. It is a notebook, kept in the open, mainly for my own students. If you find something useful, take it.
