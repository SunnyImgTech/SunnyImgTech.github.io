01 · Research

Research Areas

Broadly, five threads have run through this work for a decade — domain adaptation, saliency, fine-grained retrieval, small object detection and 3D perception. The last three years added six more, from model compression to image restoration. 51 papers in 11 areas, newest first in each; my name is in bold.
